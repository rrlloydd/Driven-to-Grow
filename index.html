My story

From car enthusiast to content creator.

The best portfolio explains not only what happened, but what you tried, what changed and what you learned.

Why I started

Cars were already something I loved, so I began sharing that interest on TikTok. Over time, creating content became a way to connect with other enthusiasts and learn how to make posts more engaging.

I'm building this page to document the process honestly — including the experiments that worked and the ones that didn't.

What I'm learning

  • How to open videos with a clear reason to keep watching.
  • How to make car content recognisable and consistent.
  • How to test different formats instead of guessing.
  • How to learn from analytics without treating every post the same.
My approach

Four things I pay attention to.

These are the principles behind my process, not a promise that every post will go viral.

01 / HOOK

Earn the first second

Give viewers a clear reason to stop scrolling, whether it's a question, a detail or a strong opening shot.

02 / VALUE

Make it worth watching

Share a useful detail, relatable opinion, story or satisfying moment for the car community.

03 / TEST

Try one change at a time

Compare formats, lengths, captions or openings so you can learn what may be influencing results.

04 / REVIEW

Use the analytics

Review available metrics such as views, watch time, shares, saves and follows rather than judging by views alone.

05 / REPEAT

Build on patterns

When a format performs well, test a fresh version that keeps the useful parts without copying another creator.

06 / COMMUNITY

Talk with your audience

Use genuine comments and questions to find future topics and make the page feel like a community.
